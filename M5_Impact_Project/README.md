# Impact Project: New Website For Beaches Water Co-op

Find a bottleneck in a real community and build an asset to minimize that problem for community members.

**Target Skills: Stakeholder Identification, Problem Solving, Web Design, Accessible Communication**

08/02/26

## Task

I found a bottleneck in the community served by the Beaches Water Co-op, located in Calvert County, Maryland. The asset I built, a new website design, allows community members to more effectively engage with the organization through their website. The new design is responsive such that it can be viewed on all screen sizes, more accessible, and modernized.

## Process

First, I reached out to the business manager of Beaches Water Co-op to propose the project and outline the improvements I would be making to the site. These improvements include making the website responsive so that it is usable on all screen sizes, modernizing its appearance, and improving the accessibility of the site with greater color contrast, skip to content links on all pages, and other considerations. I asked for features they would like to add to their site and maintained open communication with the business manager throughout the entire development process to ensure the needs of the community were being met by the new design.

My first step in the development process was to create wireframes for the website. The wireframe files are located in the folder for the impact project.

After receiving feedback from the organization on the wireframes, I started to code the site itself, implementing all features that were desired by the stakeholders, including modifications to page content and a scrolling alert bar across the top of the page to alert users about boil water advisories and other immediate communications. I provided the business manager with a draft of the home page and a few content pages for the site to receive feedback before completing the design. Using that feedback, I finalized the design for all pages of the site.

Through this project, I communicated with the business manager of Beaches Water Co-op to identify the needs of community members and build a product centered around their experiences. I incorporated feedback from the stakeholders along the way and created a product that the business manager was excited about. I also developed my technical skills by experimenting with new features and researching best practices for web design, making this experience valuable in terms of both technical and soft skills.
